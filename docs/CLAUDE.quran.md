# The platform CLAUDE.md's Quran rules (at 84aca260)

Lines 80–87 of the platform's `CLAUDE.md`, unchanged: the rules every change to the Quran code follows.

- The Quran (`modules/Quran`, sits on top: nothing depends on it; docs/architecture/02, section 26): everything of the Quran lives here, switched on per academy by its SaaS plan (`Feature::Quran`; abilities `quran.manage`/`quran.board`/`quran.teach`/`quran.examine`). Without it (`Support\QuranArea::on()`) its routes answer 403 `feature-not-in-plan` (`plan.feature:quran` before `can:`), its screens leave the menus (`canAccess`), nothing new is written, the contracts it binds answer as their defaults, and its data stays for when it comes back. The modules below reach it only through their own contracts with do-nothing defaults: Learning's `LearningQuran` (`NoLearningQuran`: `homework.expected`, a Learning column whose meaning and checks are the Quran's; each recording's check for the teacher; the report form's fields; the way from `StudentProgress` to the course's hifz map) and `JourneyQuran` (`NoJourneyQuran`: `hifz` on `GET learning/journey/map`), SharedKernel's `Quran\LessonQuran` (`NoLessonQuran`: the panel beside the room on the class page; Classroom gives only its lesson channel and viewer through `Classroom\Contracts\ClassPage`) and `Text\QuranSource` (the console's copy). Its routes keep the paths the released app and portal call (`learning/hifz…`, `learning/lessons/{session}/mushaf`, `learning/mistakes`, `teacher/students/{enrollment}/hifz…`, `teacher/texts/quran`); anything new goes under `quran/…`. Its tables keep their names (`hifz_*`, `lesson_mushaf`, `recitation_mistakes`, `submission_checks`), plus `quran_lesson_passages` (a curriculum lesson's passage by surah and verses, only through `SetLessonPassage`) and `quran_settings` (`hifz_daily_pages`). A subject is `general` or `quran` with a `quran_track` (hifz, tilawa, talqin, noorani; Catalog's `subjects.kind`, only through `SaveSubject`, the Quran only while the plan has it; `CatalogApi::subjectKind`/`subjectsOfKind`), and `HifzMap::follows` is the plan's Quran, the platform's text, and the course's subject of the Quran kind or a learner who already has pages.
  - Text: the Quran's words come only from `Texts\QuranText` (Tanzil's simple text and metadata, fetched to the server's storage by `Texts\QuranFiles` and kept only once checked whole, through `quran:text --fetch` (`curriculum:quran` is its older name), the deploy, or the console by `Text\QuranSource`; else `modules/Quran/resources/quran`; never typed, generated or changed; without the files the Quran is not offered), read through `Contracts\QuranApi` (`VerseRange`, `QuranPassage`, `QuranPage`).
  - Recitation check: recited homework (`accepts` audio or any) may say what to recite (`homework.expected` as `{kind: quran, surah, from, to}`, through `LearningQuran::recitationTarget`, checked by `Recitation\RecitationTarget`: verses there, at most `WordAlignment::MAX_EXPECTED` words, a new passage only while `recitation_check` is on); each recording handed in for it gets one `submission_checks` row, made on the `assist` queue (`QueueRecitationCheck` → `CheckRecitationJob` → `CheckRecitation`) through SharedKernel's `Ai\RecitationEngine` (`SpeechRecitation`: speech to text told only the language, then `Text\WordAlignment` against the platform's words; another engine binds the same contract), never changes the recording, grade, status, stars or KPIs, and is shown to the teacher alone (`RecitationPresenter` in teacher answers and the panel's marking modal; never `LearningPresenter`, which families share).
  - Hifz map: a learner's pages of the Madinah mushaf (604, parts from Tanzil's metadata through `QuranApi::quranPages`/`quranPage`/`quranPagesOf`; nothing shows without the platform's Quran text) live in `hifz_pages` (one per learner and page, learning or memorized, with spaced-repetition stability, difficulty and `due_at` from the pure `Domain\Hifz\Scheduler`; strength strong/fading/weak is worked out when shown, never stored). Every review is kept once in `hifz_reviews` (unique by source, its id and page): the teacher's word (`MarkMemorized`, `ClearHifzPages`), a report on a lesson with a passage (`HifzFromLessons::reportSubmitted`, by outcome), a recited homework's check (`HifzFromLessons::recited`, by the words said right on each page), and the daily review (`GradeHifzPage`, the learner's own word once a day; `ReciteHifzPage` → `CheckHifzRecitationJob` → `CheckHifzRecitation` while `recitation_check` is on, the recording deleted once heard). The daily review is `Queries\Hifz\HifzMap::wird` (due by the end of the learner's day on their clock, oldest first, at most `quran_settings.hifz_daily_pages`, saved through `SaveHifzDailyPages` in the Quran › Quran settings page); the journey's map carries `hifz: {due, done_today, daily}` (`JourneyQuran`). Learner and guardian: `FamilyHifz` (`/my`, kids in their own words), `GET learning/hifz…`, the portal and the app; the course's teacher: `Filament\Teacher\StudentHifz` (`/teach/students/{enrollment}/hifz`) and `GET teacher/students/{enrollment}/hifz…`, only on a course the map belongs to (`follows` in the answer). In /my the hifz page sorts after messages, so the kids' and middle school's tab bar keeps its tabs. Supervisors (`quran.board`) read the academy's hifz in /admin › the Quran (`Filament\Pages\HifzOverview` from `Queries\Hifz\HifzBoard`, numbers read each time; a student's map and notebook in `HifzStudent` through `Queries\Hifz\StudentHifz`, which the teacher's page uses too). Screens follow the family's `hifz` signal (`lms_notify_family` from both tables).
  - Program and plans: the academy's programs (`quran_programs`: track hifz/tilawa/talqin/noorani/review/ijaza, scope surah:verse to surah:verse (Noorani: lessons), forward or backward, unit verses/lines/page/quarter_hizb and the new amount, near review in portions, far review in pages, pass grade, exam stops, certificate) are checked by the pure `Domain\Programs\ProgramRules`, start from `Presets`, and are saved only through `SaveQuranProgram` (audited by name and field names; a program's shape is locked once a plan is on it; archived, never deleted). A course of a Quran subject (or its group, members sharing it) has one live `quran_plans` row, started only through `StartQuranPlan` (placement through `MarkMemorized`, start at the first verse not on a memorized page) and changed through `AdjustQuranPlan` (pace, position; audited by field names); without a plan it has no portion. Each lesson's portion is the pure `Domain\Programs\LessonPortion` over `Programs\Mushaf` (numbers only from the platform's copy: pages, parts, quarters, words a verse), read through `Programs\Plans` (frozen while the course is paused or over, or the group ended). A lesson is recorded only through `RecordQuranLesson` (`quran_lesson_results`, one per lesson and student, never changed): mistakes by kind (else the lesson's mushaf marks) graded by the pure `Domain\Grading` (the academy's weighted `mistake_types` and `grade_scale`; the teacher's own grade needs a note), the position moves only past a new part that meets the pass grade (a group's: most of its students) and never while frozen, and the hifz map hears each page once (source `lesson`). Families read plans and grades (`GET quran/plan`) never the teacher's note; the teacher `GET quran/lessons/{session}/portion`, `POST …/result` (idempotent), `GET`/`PATCH quran/students/{enrollment}/plan`. Settings (`quran_settings`: riwaya, mushaf, default amounts, daily pages, mistake types, grade scale, threshold, automatic recitation check, exams, certificates) change only through `SaveQuranSettings` (audited by field names).
  - Exams and certificates: `quran_exams` (a stop of the plan's program: a part or surah by `exam_stops`, the whole `program`, or `khatma`; suggested → booked → passed/failed, or cancelled) are suggested only through `SuggestQuranExam` (from `RecordQuranLesson` once a passing new part moves the plan past a stop, while `exams_enabled`; one open a plan, student and stop, held by the database), booked only through `BookQuranExam` as an `assessment` lesson through Scheduling's `Contracts\AssessmentLessons` (`BookAssessmentLesson`: free, `CourseDays::assertOpen`, `MinorsGuard`, no double booking; no lesson report) with an examiner `Domain\Exams\Examiners` allows by `quran_settings.examiner` (never the student; never the course's teacher unless `any_teacher`), recorded only by that examiner through `RecordQuranExam` (`POST quran/exams/{exam}/result`, idempotent; `Domain\Grading` over the stop's pages, passed at the program's pass grade, a fail needs a note and a retake day within 180 days and suggests the retake for it) and cancelled through `CancelQuranExam` (its lesson too); audited by the stop only. A pass earns `quran_certificates` through `IssueQuranCertificate` while `certificates_enabled` and the program `counts_for_certificate`: the academy's next number under a lock (`Q-2026-000123`), a random 16-character code (`Domain\Certificates\CertificateNumber`), what it is for as at issue; never changed but revoked once (`RevokeQuranCertificate`, a reason, audited by number; model and trigger). Its page is the signed `quran.certificate` (`Certificates\CertificateLinks`, the academy's branding, a QR drawn on the server to the portal's `/{locale}/certificates/{code}`); anyone verifies it at `GET v1/quran/certificates/{code}` (`throttle:10,1`: title, portion, grade, day, the academy, the student's first name and last initial, revoked or not). Examiners read `GET quran/examiner/exams`; families `GET quran/exams` and `GET quran/certificates` (`shown` by `certificates_family`; the kids' look without counts). Messages `quran.exam_*` and `quran.certificate_issued` (the family's own signed page). Erasure keeps an issued certificate's number, revoked, and blanks its exam; the rest go.
  - Screens: /admin › the Quran (`quran.board`; writes need `quran.manage`): an overview first (`QuranHomePage` from `Queries\Board\QuranHome`; on track or behind is the pure `Domain\Programs\Pace`), Students (`QuranStudents`: the 30-part strip `Programs\JuzStrip`, start and adjust a plan), Programs and the builder (`QuranProgramBuilder`: presets, scope strip, a typical week from `Programs\WeekPreview` through `LessonPortion`), Exams (book, cancel, issue), the examiner's `QuranExamResult` (also in /teach), Certificates (revoke) and Quran settings (through `SaveQuranSettings`). /teach: «حلقات القرآن» (`QuranLessons`) and «حصة قرآن» (`QuranLesson`: the three parts, counters from the mushaf's marks, `RecordQuranLesson`). /my: `FamilyHifz` is «القرآن» (`nav.{stage}`). The console's Quran page (`QuranConsole`: the text, fetch for owners, academies with the Quran from Platform's tables). The portal (`/family/quran`, `/teach/lessons/{id}/quran`, `/teach/students/{enrollment}/quran`, `/teach/exams`, the public `/{locale}/certificates/{code}`; Quran reads through `lib/quran/server.ts`, which shows a calm card while the plan has no Quran) and the app (`features/quran`; entries hidden on 403) read the same API. "Start here" has `quran.programs` (`ConditionalSetupStep`: only while the plan has the Quran). The demo's programs, plans, results, a passed exam with its certificate and one suggested exam come from `DemoQuranPlansSeeder` (also in `DemoCatchUpSeeder`); `E2eResetSeeder` hands the portal's `quran.spec.ts` its people, lesson and certificate.
  - Shared mushaf and mistakes notebook (the room is Classroom's): a lesson of a course the hifz map follows has one mushaf (`Mushaf\MushafLessons`: its teacher turns and marks, its student and their guardians follow and read; a hidden supervisor has none). The teacher's page is `lesson_mushaf` (`TurnMushafPage`); a mark is a word by surah, verse and place in the verse from 1 as the platform's copy counts it (`recitation_mistakes`, wrong/hesitation/tajweed, one a word a lesson, only through `MarkMistake`/`UnmarkMistake`, from the room's opening until the report is handed in or a day after the end). Pages come from `Queries\Mushaf\LessonMushaf` (words and the basmala from the platform's copy, never typed; earlier marks on the page faint), the notebook from `Queries\Mushaf\MistakesBook` (six months, by surah and verse, no teacher's name; the kids' look its own words, no counts or dates). The report handed in reviews each marked page once (`mistakes` source) and its outcome does not count that page again; `RecitationTarget::fromMistakes` is the report's `mistakes_suggestion`. Classroom reaches it only through `LessonQuran::classPanel` (its page and changes at `class/{session}/mushaf`, `Mushaf\ClassMushaf`). Sync: in a LiveKit room the teacher publishes `{v:1}` on topic `mushaf` after a change is saved and receivers act only on a sender whose server-signed metadata says `teacher`; every lesson also has its own channel `private-lesson.{session}` (seat holders; the class page signs it itself at `class/{session}/live/auth` for link visitors) and the people's `mushaf` signal; beside a mushaf, an outside meeting opens in a tab of its own. The font is Amiri Quran (OFL; the licence file sits beside it in each app).
